## Executive Summary

The open-source text-to-speech ecosystem has reached production-grade maturity in 2025-2026, with multiple models now competitive with proprietary systems like ElevenLabs and OpenAI TTS. Maya1's position within this landscape reflects a strategic trade-off: it prioritizes emotional expressiveness and zero-shot voice design over raw speed and multilingual capabilities. While Maya1 excels in certain dimensions, seven open-source models present competitive alternatives depending on specific use case requirements: Kokoro-82M (speed champion), Chatterbox (voice cloning leader), Orpheus (production scalability), Qwen3-TTS (streaming latency), Fish Speech 1.5 (multilingual quality), CosyVoice 2 (streaming architecture), and XTTS v2 (multilingual maturity).
